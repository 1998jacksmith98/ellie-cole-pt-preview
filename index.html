About me

I’m Ellie.

I help women build strength and confidence without making fitness feel restrictive. Whether you’re new to training or looking for something that actually fits your life, I keep it enjoyable and sustainable.

Whether you’re new to the gym, looking to change your physique, training towards a challenge or simply wanting to feel your best, I want to help you find a way of training that you genuinely enjoy and that fits into your life.

I offer 1:1 coaching and bootcamps, plus group classes like Burn, Kettlebell Power and Arms & Abs. I’ve been running Ellie Cole PT for over 5 years.

The goal is simple — make fitness something you enjoy enough to stick with.
