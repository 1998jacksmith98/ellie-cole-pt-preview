## Method (short)

Anonymous only: insta-stories-viewer.com (socket profile + latest 12 posts, including 3 pinned), Instagram `media/?size=l` for full JPEGs, yt-dlp 2026.08.19 for captions, carousel children and H.264 format 3, Linktree `__NEXT_DATA__`. Direct Instagram / instaloader were login-walled. Highlights, older grid (1,001 posts) and several public viewers (Picuki, Dumpor, embed) did not yield more shortcodes without a login.
